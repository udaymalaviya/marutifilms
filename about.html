About Studio

Maruti Films is a creative film and photography studio founded by Hitesh Prajapati. We believe every celebration has a story, and our job is to tell it beautifully. From intimate pre-wedding shoots to grand wedding films, corporate events and ad films, we bring cinematic storytelling, honest emotion and careful editing to every frame.

Every project is led personally by Hitesh, from the first planning call to the final colour grade. That means you always know who is behind the camera, and your film carries one consistent creative vision.

What we shoot: wedding films & highlights, pre-wedding shoots, engagement and family functions, birthdays & events, corporate films, ad films and music videos.
